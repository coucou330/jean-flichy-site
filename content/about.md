Jean Flichy is a final-year student in the Bachelor of Global Engineering, a dual degree between CentraleSupélec and McGill University, specialising in electrical engineering. He is part of the programme's first cohort. He co-leads McGill Robotics and is looking for a master's programme in energy systems from September 2027.

I like problems where the physics, the schedule and the people all matter at once. So far that has meant modelling Québec's transmission grid, coordinating trades on an offshore converter station, testing weather forecasts for a cargo airship and designing a sailboat that steers itself.
