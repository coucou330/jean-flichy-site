**TL;DR** — McGill Robotics is a student design team of 220 members building three competition robots. As Team Captain, I co-lead it with a co-director: we manage a CAD 100k budget, back in the black for the first time after a CAD 30k deficit. Before taking the role, I designed the filter shield for the underwater vehicle's hydrophones, validated in simulation (MATLAB, LTspice); tests are under way.
