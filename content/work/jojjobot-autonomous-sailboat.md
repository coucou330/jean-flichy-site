**TL;DR** — I led a team of four on a small sailboat designed to head for a GPS target on wind power alone. I integrated the electronics (Raspberry Pi, GPS, compass, wind sensors, servos) and wrote the embedded Python code; the control law comes from a published nonlinear model of a sailboat. The L2S laboratory has continued the project after us.
