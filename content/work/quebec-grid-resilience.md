**TL;DR** — With three teammates, I am building an open tool that tells which transmission corridors in Québec, if they fail in an ice or wind storm, cause the most load to be shed — and which measures reduce that risk most. So far I have extended an open PyPSA model of Hydro-Québec's network down to 315 kV and made it converge in AC power flow, cross-checked with pandapower.
