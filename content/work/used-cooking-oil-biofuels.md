**TL;DR** — Used cooking oil has become a key feedstock for European biodiesel, and allegations of fraud were appearing in the press. In my first research internship, I drafted a market overview for Prof. Olivier Massol — market structure, Europe–China trade flows, the EU Renewable Energy Directives and fraud risks — to support a paper on price links between used cooking oil and the biodiesel made from it.

**Publication.** The paper my work supported was released as a Climate Economics Chair working paper in May 2025, with an acknowledgement of my research assistance: E. Civel, O. Massol and A. Thomas, [*Unfair Trade in the Circular Economy? Price Dynamics in Chinese and European Waste to Biofuel Industries*](https://www.chaireeconomieduclimat.org/en/publications-en/unfair-trade-in-the-circular-economy-price-dynamics-in-chinese-and-european-waste-to-biofuel-industries/), Working Paper 2025-12.
