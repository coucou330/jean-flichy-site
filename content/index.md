why_energy: |
  A power cut at home was the first time I realised how much I take electricity for granted. Reading Jean-Marc Jancovici and the Shift Project's plan for the French economy then showed me that the energy transition is a question of orders of magnitude — and I wanted to understand those numbers well enough to argue with them.

  Since then I have chosen projects close to that question. My capstone models how Québec's grid holds up in an ice storm, and my last internship was on a platform that will bring more than a gigawatt of offshore wind onto the French grid. What I enjoy most, each time, is the moment when an analysis has to become a decision.
