Work

JojjoBot — autonomous sailboat

A small sailboat designed to steer itself to a target using only the wind

Dates
January – July 2025
Where
L2S laboratory (CNRS – CentraleSupélec – Université Paris-Saclay), Year 2 research project
Team
Four students
My role
Project lead; electronics and embedded software
Supervisor
Prof. Thiago Alves Lima
Tools
Python, Raspberry Pi, Simulink
  • Robotics
  • Control
  • Embedded systems

TL;DR — I led a team of four on a small sailboat designed to head for a GPS target on wind power alone. I integrated the electronics (Raspberry Pi, GPS, compass, wind sensors, servos) and wrote the embedded Python code; the control law comes from a published nonlinear model of a sailboat. The L2S laboratory has continued the project after us.
